SUNROOOF Is Not A Stretch Ceiling
AND WE TAKE OFFENCE WHEN SOMEONE CALLS IT THAT
Stretch ceilings or stretch fabrics are simply large light sources made by placing LED 
lights above a stretched fabric. The purpose of stretch ceilings is not to mimic sunlight, 
rather to just illuminate a space with light, the effect feels flat and dull.  
SUNROOOF isn’t just a light, it’s a wellness lighting technology designed with only one 
purpose - to bring sunlight indoors. It uses advanced patented technology like optics, 
lenses, and glass to recreate the feeling of real sunlight coming from above. The result is 
a beautiful sky on your ceiling, with a boost in mood, focus, and overall well-being.
